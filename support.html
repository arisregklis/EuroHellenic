What we actually do

More than an application. A supported beginning.

Plenty of agents will forward your documents to a college and collect a commission when you enrol. That is a transaction, not a service. Here is the difference, stage by stage.

Complete guidance

Our work does not stop at the offer letter.

The enrolment is the middle of the process, not the end of it. The visa, the housing, the first month and the first set of deadlines are where students actually struggle — and where most agencies have already stopped answering emails.

Start with a free conversation →
01
Assessment & shortlist

We read your transcript honestly and tell you which routes are realistic, including when the answer is “not this year”.

02
Application guidance

Documents, personal statement, references, deadlines. Prepared properly the first time, because a resubmission costs an intake.

03
Visa file preparation

The funds evidence, insurance wording, apostille and translation chain, and the sequencing that keeps it all valid at the same time.

04
Accommodation support

Where to look, what a Greek rental contract commits you to, what a deposit should be, and how to avoid the scams aimed at arrivals.

05
Arrival preparation

A plain checklist for your first fortnight: permit appointment, AFM, AMKA, bank, phone, and what month one really costs.

06
Academic support, in-house

English, IELTS, IGCSE, A Levels, maths and physics — taught by us, not subcontracted to whoever is cheapest.

The part that makes us different

We can also teach you
the thing you are missing.

Most students who get turned away are not incapable. They are half an IELTS band short, or missing a maths prerequisite. An agent can only tell you that. A teacher can fix it.

Most requested

IELTS preparation

Targeted at the band you need, with honest diagnosis of how many weeks it will realistically take. Not a generic course.

School qualifications

IGCSE & A Levels

For students completing or retaking British curriculum qualifications to meet a specific entry requirement.

Foundations

Maths & physics

The usual blockers for engineering and computing entry. Fixed properly, they stay fixed for the whole degree.

Academic English

Writing & study skills

Essay structure, referencing and academic register — the skills UK-validated assessment actually marks you on.

Handoff note Add tutoring pricing here before launch — hourly rate, package options, and whether sessions are online, in person or both. Publishing a price filters out time-wasters and signals confidence. Vague pricing costs more enquiries than a high price does.

How we are paid

The conflict of interest,
stated up front.

Every education agent in the world has one. Very few of them mention it. We would rather you weigh our advice knowing exactly how it is funded.

Free to you

The first conversation, your assessment, and your shortlist. No charge, no obligation, no pressure to proceed.

Commission, disclosed

Where an institution pays us a placement commission, we tell you which ones before you apply — so you can discount our enthusiasm accordingly.

Tutoring, quoted

Teaching is charged separately and priced up front. We will never make tutoring a condition of a placement.

“If the only thing standing between you and a better decision is our fee, we have built the wrong business.”

Ready when you are

One conversation costs nothing.

Bring your transcript, your English score if you have one, and a realistic budget. You will leave knowing where you stand.
