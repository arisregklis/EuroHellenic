Who we are

A personal advisory service, deliberately small.

EuroHellenic exists because the gap between a family in Lagos or Manila and a college in Athens is not an information gap. It is a trust gap. Those are closed by a person, not a portal.

Experience that is actually relevant

Guidance from inside the classroom.

EuroHellenic is led by an experienced Head of English Department and Academic & Careers Advisor, with extensive work in international education, student mentoring, and preparation for IELTS, IGCSE, A Levels and advanced English qualifications.

That matters more than it might sound. Most placement advisers have never marked an essay, never sat opposite a student who missed a band by half a point, and never had to tell a family that the entry requirement is not negotiable. We have done all three, which is why our assessments tend to be blunter and more accurate than the industry standard.

Students and families here deal with a named person from first enquiry through to the first semester. Not a ticketing system, not a different account manager each month.

Handoff note Add the founder's full name, photograph, qualifications and years of experience before launch. In this industry, anonymity reads as something to hide — a named face with verifiable credentials is the single highest-converting element you can add to this page.

How we work

Four commitments we
will be held to.

These are not values-page decoration. If we break one of them, tell us, and we will fix it or refund you.

01

We will tell you no

If the programme is wrong, the timing is wrong, or Greece is wrong for your goal, you will hear it plainly. A placement that unravels in year two helps nobody, least of all our reputation.

02

We disclose how we are paid

Where an institution pays us a commission, you will know which and when — before you apply, not after you have enrolled.

03

We show our sources

Every fee, requirement and figure on this site comes from a published source and is labelled as indicative. If you ask where a number came from, you get an answer, not a reassurance.

04

We stay reachable after you arrive

The commission is paid at enrolment; the student's hardest month is the one after that. We answer in month two, month six and final year. That is the whole basis of a referral business, and referrals are how we grow.

Your data

We never sell your details

Your enquiry goes to an advisor and nowhere else. We do not pass leads to third-party agents, and we do not add you to a marketing list you did not ask for.

“The right advice is worth paying for. The wrong advice costs three years and the fees that went with them.”
