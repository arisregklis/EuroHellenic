Before you choose

Four questions worth
asking about any programme.

Ask these of us, and of every other adviser you speak to. The quality of the answers will sort the industry for you faster than any brochure.

Which university awards this exact degree?

Not the college's partners in general — this programme. Then verify it on the awarding university's own site.

What is the total cost across all years?

Including registration, examination, insurance and any placement fees. Year-one tuition is not the number that matters.

What happens if I fail a module?

Resit fees, repeat-year cost, and how it affects your residence permit renewal. Ask before you need to know.

Where do graduates of this route actually go?

Ask for destinations from the last two cohorts of this programme, not the college as a whole.
