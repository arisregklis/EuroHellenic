Where you would actually study

The institutions we introduce.

Four established options, each with a different awarding structure and a different centre of gravity. We introduce students to these; we do not pretend they are the only routes into European higher education.

Understand the structure first

Who teaches you, and who signs the certificate.

This is the single thing most families misunderstand, and the single thing that determines whether the qualification does what you need it to.

Greek private colleges operate as centres of a foreign university. The college in Athens delivers the teaching, the assessment and the student experience. The awarding university — British or American — validates the curriculum, moderates the marking, and issues the degree certificate in its own name.

So your transcript says where you studied, and your degree says who awarded it. Both are true, and both are visible to an employer. Anyone who tells you to obscure the first half is giving you bad advice.

What to verify, every time

  • 01 The awarding university names this college, and this programme, on its own website.
  • 02 The college holds current licence of operation from the Greek Ministry of Education.
  • 03 The fee quoted to you is the fee published for your intake year and nationality.
  • 04 If you will register professionally elsewhere, that regulator recognises this award.

We run these four checks before we recommend anything. You should still run them yourself.

University of Nicosia

A full university rather than a teaching centre, awarding its own degrees — a structurally different proposition from the three above, and priced accordingly. Worth the premium where you want a single institution's own award, or need programmes such as medicine that the colleges do not run.

At a glance

Which one suits which student

Choose Mediterranean College if

You want the widest English-taught choice with one clear UK awarding body, and a fee you can plan around from day one.

Choose Metropolitan College if

Your field is health sciences, law or education, or you want a specific UK partner that Metropolitan holds and others do not.

Choose New York College if

An American degree carries more weight in your home market, or the United States is part of your longer plan.

Choose University of Nicosia if

You want a university's own award rather than a franchised one, need medicine, and the higher fee is workable.
