The Greece advantage

European education.
Mediterranean cost of living.

Greece is not a compromise destination. It is a legitimate route to a British or American degree, inside the EU, at a total cost most families can actually plan for — and it has become one of the fastest-growing study destinations in southern Europe for exactly that reason.

01

Study entirely in English

Private colleges in Athens and Thessaloniki deliver full UK and US degree programmes in English under franchise and validation agreements. You are taught in Greece; the degree certificate is issued by the awarding university.

02

Spend far less to get there

Indicative undergraduate tuition starts around €6,750 a year. Compare that with £16,000–£38,000 for international students in the UK, before living costs that run roughly double.

03

Build inside the EU

A Greek student residence permit is a Schengen residence permit. You study in the EU, travel across it, and can work up to 20 hours a week during term time with the right registrations.

From first message to first lecture

Six steps. No surprises.

Start eight to ten months before your intake if you can. It is still workable at four. Below that, we will tell you to aim for the next intake rather than take your money and rush a visa file that will be refused.

01

Week one · Free

An honest first conversation

You tell us your grades, your English level and your budget. We tell you which routes are realistic and which are not. Some people leave this call deciding not to study in Greece. That is a good outcome too.

02

Weeks 2–4

Shortlist and compare

Three or four programmes side by side: awarding university, real annual cost, entry requirements, accreditation, and what graduates of that specific route tend to do next.

03

Weeks 4–8

Application and offer

Transcripts, personal statement, references, English evidence. We prepare the file properly the first time, because a resubmission costs you a whole intake.

04

Allow 1–3 months

The national D visa

Acceptance letter, proof of funds, health insurance, accommodation evidence, apostilled and translated criminal record certificate. This stage fails on paperwork, not on merit — so we are pedantic about it.

05

Before departure

Housing and arrival plan

Where to live, what a Greek rental contract commits you to, what to bring, what to budget for month one, and how to avoid the deposit scams that target new international students.

06

First weeks in Greece

Residence permit and settling in

Apply for the residence permit at the Aliens and Immigration Department, register for AFM and AMKA, open a bank account, and get your academic support in place before the first deadline lands.

Straight answers

The questions families
actually ask first

Usually some version of: is this degree real, and what is the catch? Both are fair questions. See all questions →

Yes — when the programme is delivered under a validation or franchise agreement, the awarding university issues its own degree certificate, identical to the one issued on its home campus. Your transcript records the teaching institution. What you must check, every time, is the specific agreement for the specific programme: ask which university awards it, and confirm it directly on that university's own website, not only on the college's. We do this check for every programme we put in front of you, and we will show you where to verify it yourself.
For undergraduate entry, typically IELTS 6.0 with no component below 5.5, or an accepted equivalent such as PTE Academic 50+ or Cambridge English at B2. Postgraduate entry is usually 6.0–6.5. Foundation routes can accept around IELTS 4.5. If you are half a band short, that is a teaching problem with a timeline, not a closed door — and it happens to be exactly what we specialise in.
Non-EU students holding a valid student residence permit may work up to 20 hours per week during term time. You will need an AFM (tax number) and AMKA (social security number), and the employment must be formally registered. Full-time work may be possible during official holidays — confirm with the Migration Office. Be realistic: part-time earnings in Greece will support your living costs at the margin. They will not fund your tuition, and migration authorities do monitor academic progress when you renew.
The first conversation and your shortlist cost you nothing. Where we receive a placement commission from an institution, we will tell you which institutions those are before you apply, so you can weigh our advice accordingly. Tutoring and exam preparation are charged separately and quoted up front. If anyone in this industry refuses to answer this question plainly, treat that as the answer.
