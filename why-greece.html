The case, stated plainly

Why Greece is a serious option — and where it isn't.

Greece is not the right answer for every student. It is a very good answer for a specific one. This page is about telling those two apart before you spend a year finding out.

The core argument

The same degree,
at a third of the cost.

A private college in Athens can teach a University of Derby or University of Greenwich degree under a formal franchise agreement. You sit the same assessments; the awarding university issues the certificate. What changes is the price of the years around it.

Study in Greece €0

Indicative annual undergraduate tuition for an English-taught UK degree delivered in Athens.

Study in the UK £16k–38k

Typical international undergraduate tuition range at a UK campus, before living costs roughly double those in Greece.

The difference, over three years £30k+

Money that stays with your family, or funds a master's, or removes the need for a loan you would service for a decade.

Comparison is indicative and course-dependent. UK figures vary widely by institution and subject; clinical and laboratory subjects sit at the top of that range.

Five reasons that hold up

What Greece genuinely offers

01

Language

You never need Greek to graduate

Entire programmes — lectures, assessment, dissertation, supervision — run in English at the private colleges. Greek is worth learning for your life outside the building, and for your employability afterwards, but it is not a barrier to entry or to your degree.

02

Recognition

The awarding body is a UK or US university

University of Derby, Queen Margaret University, University of Greenwich, University of East London, Oxford Brookes, Solent, University of Greater Manchester and SUNY Empire State University all award degrees taught in Greece. Your certificate carries their name. Always confirm the specific agreement for the specific programme — we will show you where.

03

Cost of living

A budget that a normal family can carry

A room in a shared Athens flat runs roughly €450–€700 a month; in Thessaloniki or Crete, meaningfully less. Total monthly living costs typically land between €700 and €1,100 outside Athens and €930–€1,480 in the capital. Groceries, transport and coffee are among the cheaper in western Europe.

04

Mobility

A Schengen residence permit

Your Greek student residence permit lets you travel across the Schengen area for short stays — conferences, summer schools, family visits, and simply seeing the continent you have moved to. For students whose longer plan is European, starting inside the union rather than outside it is not a small structural advantage.

05

Daily life

A country that is straightforward to live in

Mild winters, low violent-crime rates in the student districts, a long habit of hosting international visitors, and cities where a student can walk, eat and socialise without a car. None of this shows up on a league table. All of it determines whether you finish the degree.

Position matters

A base inside the union.

Athens sits at the southeastern corner of the EU, four hours or less from most European capitals. For students from South and Southeast Asia, it is also one of the shorter flights home in the whole union.

The part most agents skip

Where Greece is the wrong choice

If any of the following describes you, we will say so on the first call rather than at the point where you have already paid a deposit.

You need a globally ranked research university

If your goal is a top-100 research institution for a PhD track or a prestige-sensitive industry, the private colleges in Greece are not that. They are teaching institutions delivering another university's degree. That is a different product, and it should be priced and judged as one.

You are counting on a long post-study work visa

Greece does not offer a post-study work route comparable to the UK Graduate Route or Canada's PGWP. Staying on after graduation generally means securing a job offer and a corresponding permit, or continuing to further study. Plan for that from year one rather than discovering it in your final semester.

You are entering a regulated profession elsewhere

Nursing, physiotherapy, psychology and teaching are regulated differently in every country. A degree that registers you in Greece may require additional steps to register in India, Nigeria or the Gulf. Check with the destination regulator before you enrol. We will help you ask the right question.

You expect part-time work to fund your degree

Twenty hours a week at Greek wage levels will help with living costs. It will not pay tuition. Any adviser who implies otherwise is selling you a visa refusal — because the consulate will ask for proof of funds, and "I will work" is not an answer they accept.
